## 2026-11-05 - Touch Target HitSlop Learning
**Learning:** React Native applications require generous touch targets on interactive components, especially on mobile. While standard buttons tend to be adequately sized by their visual constraints, small icon-only buttons (like modal close buttons, search clear buttons, and calendar inputs) usually fail to meet minimum target guidelines, creating a frustrating experience.
**Action:** Always wrap small interactive elements like icon-only `TouchableOpacity` or `Pressable` in `hitSlop={{ top: 12, bottom: 12, left: 12, right: 12 }}` to expand the touchable area without altering the visual design.
